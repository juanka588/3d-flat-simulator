## Tone
Austere but warm: a calm, heavy, monastic space. Mass, shadow and texture instead of decoration.

## Principles
- Concrete-feel walls and floor are the stars; treat them as surfaces to be lit.
- Use two greys and one warm note (cognac leather or raw oak).
- Choose heavy, plain, monolithic furniture; fewer pieces, larger scale.
- Light grazes the wall to reveal texture; keep it warm and low.
- Leave generous empty floor and wall.

## Séjour / cuisine
Concrete grey walls with one darker wall behind the sofa. Floor: keep the parquet but cover a large part with a heavy grey rug, or use a micro-cement floor zone. Block sofa in anthracite, slab coffee table, one cognac leather chair, steel shelving. Dining: heavy wood table with black chairs under a single dark pendant. Keep the kitchen tiles.

## Chambres
Low wide bed in dark wood or grey, grey and bone bedding, charcoal curtains, a slab nightstand. One concrete wall behind the bed. Second room as a work room: steel shelving, a plain desk.

## Entrée
A steel or black console, no mirror ornament - a plain rectangular mirror; keep the 0.9 m walkway.

## Salle de bain
Keep tiles and fittings. Add a black mirror frame, grey towels, one stone object.

## WC
A black frame print or none; one grey towel.

## Balcon
Two black steel chairs, a small stone-look table, a single large plant in a concrete pot.

## Do
- Let wall texture show: grazing light.
- Repeat the same grey family across surfaces.
- Keep decor to one or two sculptural objects per room.

## Don't
- No ornament, patterns or pastels.
- Don't fill the room; mass needs space.
- No cold white light.
