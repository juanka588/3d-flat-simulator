## Tone
Glamorous, confident and theatrical, but tailored: an evening at a 1925 Paris hotel bar, cut to a flat.

## Principles
- One jewel-tone feature wall (emerald or navy) per main room; champagne for the rest.
- Strict symmetry: pairs of lamps, mirrors, chairs.
- Brass and black define the lines; velvet softens them.
- A mirror per room to multiply light.
- Geometric motifs only in one or two places: rug, mirror, lamp.

## Séjour / cuisine
Champagne walls and an emerald feature wall behind a velvet sofa. A sunburst mirror above it, a brass-and-glass coffee table, a pair of brass lamps, a geometric rug. Black table with velvet chairs under a globe pendant. Keep the kitchen tiles; add brass bar stools and a mirrored tray.

## Chambres
Navy or emerald behind a velvet-headboard bed, symmetrical nightstands with brass lamps, satin bedding, velvet curtains. The second room: a black lacquer desk and a velvet chair, a gold-framed mirror.

## Entrée
A black console with brass feet, a large arch or sunburst mirror, a pair of lamps.

## Salle de bain
Keep tiles and fittings. Add a brass-framed mirror, dark towels, brass accessories.

## WC
A small gold-framed print, a brass hook, a candle.

## Balcon
Black metal bistro set with a brass-look lantern and a palm in a dark pot.

## Do
- Keep every composition symmetrical.
- Repeat brass at least four times per main room.
- Use velvet for at least two large surfaces.

## Don't
- No rustic, boho or distressed wood.
- No pastel palettes or cold white light.
- Don't overload: each room has one hero.
