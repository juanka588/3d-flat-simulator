## Tone
Light, practical, kind. A flat that feels bigger and calmer in a grey winter.

## Principles
- Maximise daylight: pale walls, sheer curtains, mirrors opposite windows.
- A restrained palette of white, wood and one muted accent (sage or dusty blue).
- Natural materials and tactile textiles carry the warmth.
- Furniture is simple, light on its legs and multi-purpose.
- Layer many small, warm light sources (hygge).

## Séjour / cuisine
Soft white walls. A light sofa on a big off-white rug, nesting coffee tables in birch, an open shelf unit, a floor lamp. A wooden dining table with mismatched pale chairs under a paper pendant. Keep the kitchen tiles; add wooden boards and ceramic jars.

## Chambres
Calm and low: a birch bed, linen bedding in white and grey, wool throw, two small bedside tables with ceramic lamps. One wall in sage or dusty blue. The second room works as a bright study with a white desk and a wood chair.

## Entrée
A slim bench with shoe storage, a row of hooks, a round mirror; light wood.

## Salle de bain
Keep the tiles and fittings. Add a wooden stool, a plant, white towels in a stack, a simple mirror.

## WC
A small plant and a frame; white or sage towels.

## Balcon
Two folding wooden chairs, a small table, a pot with herbs and a wool throw in a basket.

## Do
- Leave breathing space: empty wall is fine.
- Repeat wood tone in three or more pieces.
- Use texture (wool, linen) rather than pattern.

## Don't
- No dark leather, no chrome-and-glass, no gold.
- Don't hang more than two frames per wall.
- Don't use cold white bulbs.
