## Tone
Urban, workmanlike and relaxed: a former workshop with comfortable leather and warm light.

## Principles
- One brick or dark accent wall; the rest light concrete grey.
- Black steel appears as thin lines (frames, legs, lamps) - not as bulky blocks.
- Warm, worn wood and leather offset the cold metals.
- Show the structure and the light source: pendants low, bulbs visible.
- Open shelving rather than closed cabinets.

## Séjour / cuisine
Concrete-grey walls, a brick accent behind the sofa or the TV. A leather sofa, a steel-and-wood coffee table, an open steel bookcase, a worn rug. A trestle dining table with metal chairs, two or three factory pendants. Keep the kitchen tiles; add metal stools at the island.

## Chambres
A black-metal or dark-wood bed, grey and charcoal bedding, one exposed-bulb bedside lamp each side, an open clothes rail if space allows. The second room: a steel-and-wood desk with an adjustable lamp.

## Entrée
A steel bench or a rail with hooks, a black-framed mirror, a metal shoe rack.

## Salle de bain
Keep tiles and fittings. Black accessories, a wooden stool, a round black-framed mirror.

## WC
A black frame print, a metal hook, a plant.

## Balcon
Metal bistro chairs and table, galvanised planters.

## Do
- Repeat black steel three times per room.
- Pair every cold metal with something warm (wood, leather, wool).
- Hang pendants at 60-70 cm over tables.

## Don't
- No pastels, no white gloss, no ornament.
- Don't fake the brick on every wall.
- No cold bulbs.
