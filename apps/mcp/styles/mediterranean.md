## Tone
Relaxed, sunny and hospitable: lunch on a terrace that never ends.

## Principles
- Whitewashed walls reflect the light; colour comes from terracotta, olive and sea blue in small doses.
- Natural, hand-made materials, a bit irregular.
- Wicker and rope for lightness; rustic wood for weight.
- Plenty of plants, especially in terracotta.
- Indoors and outdoors blend: keep the balcony door visible and inviting.

## Séjour / cuisine
White walls, an arched-look shelf or niche if the catalogue allows; a linen sofa, a rattan armchair, a rustic wood coffee table, a jute rug, a big terracotta pot. A rustic dining table with wicker chairs and a rattan pendant. Keep the kitchen tiles; add blue-and-white ceramics.

## Chambres
Wood or rattan bed, white and blue linen bedding, a woven pendant or lamp, linen curtains. One wall in soft terracotta or sea blue behind the bed. The second room: a daybed or desk with a wicker chair.

## Entrée
A wooden bench, a woven basket, a round rattan or wood-framed mirror, hooks.

## Salle de bain
Keep tiles and fittings. Blue-and-white towels, a wooden stool, a terracotta pot with greenery.

## WC
A blue ceramic dish, a plant, a woven basket.

## Balcon
A bistro table and two wicker or wood chairs, lanterns, terracotta pots with herbs and an olive-tree plant.

## Do
- Use at most two bold colours per room.
- Group terracotta pots in threes.
- Choose linen over synthetic fabrics.

## Don't
- No chrome, glass-and-steel or dark grey.
- No seashell or anchor kitsch.
- Don't hide the light with heavy curtains.
