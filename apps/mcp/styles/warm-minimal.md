## Tone
Calm, modern and welcoming. A flat that looks good in photographs and feels good at 9 pm.

## Principles
- Soft warm white on most walls; one or two muted accent walls (sage, clay or greige), never more.
- Oak, linen and matt ceramics as the material trio.
- Few, well-chosen pieces; every surface has room to breathe.
- Layered warm light: grid, pendant, floor and table lamps, all 2700-3000 K.
- Finish with decor on surfaces using the `on` relation, plus rugs, plants and a mirror.

## Séjour / cuisine
Warm white walls, the TV or sofa wall in sage or greige. Sofa, round oak coffee table, wool rug, TV unit on the TV wall, floor lamp, plants. Island or dining table with stools or chairs and a pendant. Keep the kitchen tiles; add a tray, a bowl and plants.

## Chambres
Oak bed, linen bedding, two nightstands with lamps, a rug, linen curtains. One accent wall behind the bed (clay or sage). The second room can be a guest room or study with a desk and chair.

## Entrée
A slim shoe cabinet or console (25-35 cm deep), a wall mirror and hooks. Keep 0.9 m clear.

## Salle de bain
Keep the existing tiles and fittings. Add a mirror, towels, a plant and a stool if space allows.

## WC
A small print or shelf, a plant, a hook.

## Balcon
A bistro table with two chairs and a couple of planters.

## Do
- Keep a maximum of two accent colours.
- Place lamps that emit light in every room.
- Check evening renders: warm and cosy, no dark corners.

## Don't
- No strong patterns or neon colours.
- Don't fill every wall.
- No cold white bulbs.
