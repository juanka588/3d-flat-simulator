## Tone
Warm, free-spirited and lived-in: a well-travelled friend's flat full of plants and stories.

## Principles
- Layer textures and patterns, but tie them with 3-4 earthy colours.
- Mix furniture eras and materials; keep one wood tone dominant.
- Bring plants in at every height, including hanging.
- Keep seating low and relaxed; add poufs and floor cushions.
- Cosy lighting: many small warm sources.

## Séjour / cuisine
Warm cream walls, a terracotta or mustard accent. A deep sofa with patterned cushions, a rattan armchair, a layered kilim-on-jute rug, a woven pouf, a macramé on the wall. Wooden dining table with rattan chairs under a woven pendant. Keep the kitchen tiles; add baskets, clay pots and a hanging planter.

## Chambres
Rattan or wooden bed, block-print bedding, many cushions, a woven pendant, a plant in the corner. One sage or terracotta wall. The second room: a floor-cushion reading nook or a desk with a cane chair.

## Entrée
A bench with baskets, a rattan mirror, hooks with bags and hats, a plant.

## Salle de bain
Keep tiles and fittings. Hanging plant, woven basket, colourful towels, a rattan mirror.

## WC
A macramé hanging or print and a trailing plant.

## Balcon
Floor cushions, a low table, hanging planters and string lights, lanterns.

## Do
- Group plants in odd numbers.
- Layer at least two rugs in the living room.
- Mix three patterns of different scale.

## Don't
- No matching furniture sets.
- No sterile white or glossy surfaces.
- Don't use more than four main colours.
