## Tone
Quiet, grounded, deliberate. Every object earns its place.

## Principles
- Subtract first: fewer, better pieces and open floor.
- Keep furniture low (seat height 35-40 cm for lounge pieces) to make ceilings feel higher.
- Natural, slightly imperfect materials; two wood tones at most.
- Neutral earthy palette with one muted green.
- Soft, indirect light only.

## Séjour / cuisine
Sand and rice-white walls. A low linen sofa, a round or rectangular low wood coffee table, a jute rug, a paper floor lantern. A wooden dining table with a bench and two chairs under a paper pendant. Keep the kitchen tiles; a single branch in a ceramic vase.

## Chambres
Low platform bed in oak, oatmeal linen bedding, a bench at the foot, one low nightstand each side, linen curtains. Keep walls bare except for one print. The second room: a small low desk and a cushion or stool.

## Entrée
A narrow wooden bench with baskets under it, hooks, a simple mirror; a single stem in a vase.

## Salle de bain
Keep tiles and fittings. Wooden stool, folded linen towels, a woven basket, a plant.

## WC
One ceramic object and a small plant; nothing else.

## Balcon
Two low wooden stools or a bench, a woven mat, a plant in a stoneware pot.

## Do
- Leave at least a third of every wall empty.
- Group objects in odd numbers on trays.
- Prefer matt and tactile finishes.

## Don't
- No bright colours, no patterns, no gloss.
- Don't line walls with tall storage.
- Don't use more than two wood tones.
