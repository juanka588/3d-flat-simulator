## Tone
A Parisian pied-à-terre: refined, a little worn-in, never showy. Think Haussmann salon scaled down to a contemporary flat.

## Principles
- Create a moulding feel with paint, not with fake plaster: panel the focal wall with a slightly deeper colour and keep the ceiling white.
- Warm chevron oak underfoot everywhere except wet rooms; layer a pale rug.
- One metal (brass) repeated in lamps, frames and handles; one stone (marble) repeated in two tabletops.
- Symmetry and a focal point per room; tall mirrors to double the daylight.
- Mix one curved piece with straight ones; keep colour for textiles and one deep accent.

## Séjour / cuisine
Ivory walls, the sofa wall in warm greige or bottle green. Velvet or boucle sofa on a pale rug, marble-look round coffee table, caned armchair, floor lamp in brass. A tall mirror on the wall facing the window. Bistro table with caned chairs in the dining zone, a brass suspension above. Keep the kitchen zone tiles; add a tray, carafe and plants.

## Chambres
Cosy and symmetrical: upholstered or wooden bed, two nightstands with matching lamps, linen curtains. Main bedroom wall in sage or greige; second bedroom as a study with a desk and a mirror. Wool rug, one framed print above the bed.

## Entrée
A slim console with a round mirror above and a small tray for keys; brass hooks. Keep the 0.9 m walkway.

## Salle de bain
Keep the existing tiles and fittings. Soften with a framed mirror, a wooden stool, white and sage towels, a brass-look accessory set.

## WC
Keep the tiles. A small framed print, a plant or candle, one brass hook.

## Balcon
Bistro set: folding table and two chairs, a pot of herbs, a string of warm lights if available.

## Do
- Repeat brass three times per room, not more.
- Hang mirrors and art high, near the cornice line.
- Mix matte stone and glossy brass.

## Don't
- No chrome, no glass-and-steel, no neon colours.
- No oversized sectional sofas.
- Don't paint the ceiling a colour.
